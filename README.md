# EG Commerce

EG Commerce is an all-in-one e-commerce marketplace built around a **content-first commerce experience**.

## Key Highlights

- 🎥 **Product Reels**: Seamless discovery driven by short-form video content.
- 🛍️ **Unified Marketplace**: Explore curated products from verified merchants.
- 🎁 **Reward System**: Incentivize loyalty, social engagement, and customer retention.
- 🤝 **UGC Opportunities**: Bridge the gap between brands and creators.
- 📊 **Commerce Attribution**: Full tracking of content impact, creator influence, and conversion paths.
